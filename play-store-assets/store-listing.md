## FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)

**English**

Learn English the easy way — by looking, not by memorising word lists.

LinguaQuiz shows you a picture and asks a simple question. Tap the right word and move on. That is the whole app. No accounts, no ads interrupting you, no internet needed.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• 10 themed levels: Fruits, Animals, Home, Family, Colors, Numbers, Weather, Food, Clothes and Everyday Conversation
• 50 picture questions with four answer options each
• Levels unlock as you progress, so you always know what to study next
• Your progress is saved automatically — close the app and continue later
• A result screen after every level showing each question, the correct answer and your answer

WHY IT WORKS
Seeing an apple and choosing the word "Apple" builds a direct link between the object and the English word. No translation step in the middle. This is how children learn their first language, and it works for beginners of any age.

PERFECT FOR
• Absolute beginners starting English from zero
• Children learning their first English words
• Anyone who wants a short daily practice that takes five minutes
• Learners who want to study on the bus, on a plane or anywhere without signal

FULLY OFFLINE
Every picture and question is stored inside the app. LinguaQuiz asks for no internet permission at all, collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.

Start with Level 1 and work your way to Level 10.
