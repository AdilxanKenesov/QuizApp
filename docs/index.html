Privacy Policy

LinguaQuiz · com.visionsystems.linguaquiz
Vision Systems Corp · Last updated: 26 August 2026
Summary: LinguaQuiz does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet access permission and works entirely offline.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. LinguaQuiz does not gather any personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, usage analytics or crash reports.

We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, phone number or any other detail about yourself.

2. Internet Access

LinguaQuiz does not request the INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. The application is technically incapable of sending data anywhere or contacting any server. Every image, question and answer is stored inside the app package on your device.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves your quiz progress — which levels you have completed and your answers — using Android's local preference storage. This information:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not readable by us or by any third party;
  • is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Third-Party Services

LinguaQuiz contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no social media integrations and no third-party tracking libraries of any kind.

5. Children's Privacy

LinguaQuiz is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it cannot collect personal information from children. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy.

6. Permissions

LinguaQuiz requests no Android runtime permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, storage, contacts, location or any other protected resource.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after such an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
